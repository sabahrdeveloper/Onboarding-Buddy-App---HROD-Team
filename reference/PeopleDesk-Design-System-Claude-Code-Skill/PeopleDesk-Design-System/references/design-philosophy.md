# Design Philosophy

PeopleDesk must feel like a human-centered HR experience, not a complex enterprise system.

Principles:

- Human-centered HR experience
- AI-assisted, not AI-first
- Low cognitive load
- Consistency over creativity
- Bangla-first experience
- Professional and friendly
- Trustworthy and supportive
- Enterprise-grade simplicity

Design goal:

Help employees complete HR tasks with confidence, clarity, and minimum confusion.

The app should not feel overly technical, flashy, experimental, or childish.
